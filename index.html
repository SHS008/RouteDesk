RouteDesk submits completed jobs to a Supabase project you own. Three steps, about five minutes, free tier.

DRIVER PROFILE

Route defaults

Used when creating a new route. Route assignments are shared through exported route files.

These free apps can provide live traffic for each individual navigation leg. RouteDesk does not reorder stops using live traffic.

iPhone shows its own “Open in Google Maps?” confirmation whenever RouteDesk hands off to another app. That dialog is drawn by iOS and cannot be turned off from inside the app. Apple Maps opens without it, and “Copy the address” never leaves RouteDesk at all.

Google Maps accepts about three intermediate stops in a single link, so a long route opens in groups of the next pending stops. Apple Maps and Waze links only carry one stop, so those navigate one at a time.
